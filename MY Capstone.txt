Project: AI Test Failure Investigation Agent

What does it do?
It helps software developers and QA engineers investigate automated test failures and identify likely root causes from test logs and project documentation.

Who is it for?
Software developers and QA engineers who investigate failed automated tests.

Most important task:
Given a failed automated test and its error log, identify the most likely cause and recommend what to check next.

Demo Day goal:
Give the agent a real or realistic test failure, have it investigate using documentation, previous failures, and tools, then produce a grounded root-cause analysis and investigation report.
